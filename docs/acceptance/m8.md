# Testing M8 (iPhone) by hand

Before you start, set up team sync with a **Cloudflare Worker** ([m7.md](m7.md) §1). The phone reaches your Mac through the Worker, so it works on cellular.

## 1. Run it

On the simulator:

```bash
cd apps/relay && flutter run -d "iPhone 17 Pro"
```

On your iPhone, open `apps/relay/ios/Runner.xcworkspace` in Xcode. Pick your team under Signing, then Run.

Without a phone: `RELAY_MOBILE=1` runs the phone UI in a Mac window (separate data folder: `RELAY_HOME=/tmp/relay-phone`).

## 2. Pair (M8-02)

1. On the Mac, open **Settings → Team → Pair iPhone…**. A QR code appears.
2. On the phone, choose **Scan the QR code**, or **Paste a code** (on the simulator: copy the code on the Mac, then run `xcrun simctl pbcopy booted` with the code on stdin). Expected:
   - **Inbox** appears with your team's name.
   - The Mac's Settings → Team → Devices lists "Rifat's iPhone".
   - The code works once.

## 3. The screens (M8-03, I01–I05)

- **Inbox:**
  - Agents asking you show up first: **Allow once / Deny** for permissions, and one-tap answers for either/or questions plus *Reply in your own words*.
  - Then **Ready for review**, then **Mentions** (`@you` in a channel).
- **Board:**
  - The project picker sits in the top-left pill.
  - **Queued / Running / Review** sit in a glass segment.
  - Live cards show the current step. Waiting cards have an orange border with **Answer**.
- **Run:** open a task.
  - **Live** lists steps as the agent works on the Mac, arriving in about a second.
  - **Plan**, **Diff** (per repo +/−; merge on the Mac) and **Thread** with a reply box.
  - Pause/stop sit top right, and you can type to steer the agent.
  - A permission request slides up as the glass sheet: Deny / Allow once / Always allow.
- **Agents:**
  - The Mac shows *Awake* while online, with its agents' slots, spend and limits.
  - Teammates' shared agents are listed below.
  - ⏸ pauses new work on the Mac.
- **Chat:** channels, then a channel: bubbles, the digest, agents' task cards with **Watch run**, and `/task @agent …` in the composer.
- **+** makes a task that runs on your Mac. 🔍 searches tasks.

Check the glass:

- The tab bar and pills blur what scrolls under them.
- Scrolling stays smooth (120 Hz on iPhone 15 Pro and later).
- At most four glass panes are on screen at once: two pills, the tab bar and search, or one sheet.

## 4. Act from the phone

1. Queue `/task @claude-code …` in a channel, or **+** → a task. It runs on the Mac (`relay tasks` there shows it).
2. When the agent asks for permission, allow it on the phone. The Mac's run continues.
3. Turn off the Mac, or quit relayd. Actions on the phone say *Rifat's MacBook Pro is offline or asleep.*

## 5. Push (M8-04)

1. In Apple Developer, go to Certificates, IDs & Profiles → **Keys** → **+**. Enable *Apple Push Notifications service*, then download `AuthKey_XXXXXXXXXX.p8`. Note your **Team ID**.
2. On the Mac, open **Settings → Team → iPhone push → Set up…**. Choose the .p8 and fill in the key ID and team ID. Or run:

   ```bash
   relay team push-setup --p8 AuthKey_X.p8 --apple-team TEAMID
   ```

3. On the phone, allow notifications at the prompt. The row shows *On · 1 phone registered*.
4. Lock the phone. When an agent asks something on the Mac, a notification arrives: *TM-12 needs your OK*. Tapping it opens the task.
5. Turn **Show task names** off. The notification then says only *An agent needs you*.

Simulator check without Apple keys: `xcrun simctl push booted com.binarycastle.relay push.json` with:

```json
{"aps": {"alert": {"title": "TM-3 has a question", "body": "…"}}, "relay": {"taskId": "<id>"}}
```

The banner shows while Relay is in the background.
